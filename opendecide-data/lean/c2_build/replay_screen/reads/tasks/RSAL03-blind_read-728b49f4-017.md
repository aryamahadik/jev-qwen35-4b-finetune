This date is on your certificate of sponsorship.\nYou should get a decision on your visa within 3 weeks when you apply from outside the UK.\nFind out about paying for a faster decision.\n\n## Application fee\nThe application fee for each person applying is £244.\nThe fee is the same whether you’re applying from inside or outside the UK.\n\n### If you’re from an eligible country\nYour application fee will be automatically reduced by £55 if you’re from one of the following countries:\nAustria, Belgium, Croatia, Republic of Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden or Turkey.\nThis reduction only applies to your visa application. Your partner and children will still need to pay the full application fee.\n\n### Healthcare surcharge\nYou’ll also have to pay the healthcare surcharge as part of your application. Check how much you’ll have to pay before you apply.\n\n### If you are applying to extend from inside the UK\nYou’ll need to pay £19.20 to have your biometric information (fingerprints and a photo) taken.\n\n### Get a faster decision on your application\nIf you’re applying to extend from inside the UK you can pay an extra £500 for the priority service to get a decision within 5 working days.\nYou can pay an extra £800 for the super priority service to get a decision:\n- by the end of the next working day after providing your biometric information if your appointment is on a weekday\n- 2 working days after providing your biometric information if your appointment is at the weekend\nWorking days are Monday to Friday, not including bank holidays.\nOnce you’ve got your decision letter, your biometric residence permit will take up to 10 working days to arrive.\n\n## How long you can stay\nYou’ll be given a visa to live and work in the UK for up to 24 months, or up to 28 days more than the time on your certificate of sponsorship. It’s not an actual certificate or paper document.\nYour sponsor will give you the certificate of sponsorship.\nYour sponsor must also give you the information they used on your certificate about your job, for example your working hours.\nYour sponsor must be recognised by the UK government to issue certificates of sponsorship.\nYour certificate of sponsorship is valid for 3 months from the date it is assigned to you.\n\n### Multiple entry\nYour sponsor can give you a multiple entry certificate of sponsorship so you can leave and return to the UK.\n\n## Money to support yourself\nYou must have at least £1,270 in your bank account to show you can support yourself in the UK.\nYou will need to have had the money available for at least 28 days in a row.
