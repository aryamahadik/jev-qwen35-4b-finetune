## 0. Read this first

1. **There is no public agent-safety TRAIN set that passes the owner rules.** Every agent-trajectory safety set found (R-Judge, ATBench, AgentDoG training data, TS-Bench, AgentAlign, StepGuard, StepSafe, AgentDrift, ASSEBench, Agent-SafetyBench, AgentHarm, OS-Harm, SABER, ClawSafety, MT-AgentRisk, BLINDSPOT, TraceSafe) is one of: test-only benchmark; LLM-generated or LLM-labelled; unreleased; or barred in the registry (E30, E31, E32, C2, C3). The plan's position (Claude-authored generators with program or execution gold for T50, T51, T53, T41c, T55, T56g) is confirmed, not weakened, by this sweep.
2. **No public tool-output prompt-injection TRAIN set is human-labelled and permissive.** The two with permissive licences and train splits that are close to our T41c shape are programmatic splices into LLM-made carriers: prodnull/prompt-injection-repo-dataset (Apache-2.0, repo files, partly LLM-paraphrased; row S52), rgeada/tool-response-injections (Apache-2.0, tool responses from an LLM-made tool-call set; S53) and BrowseSafe-Bench (MIT, HTML pages, attack text rewritten by Claude Sonnet 4.5; S51). All three are OWNER. The agent-trajectory injection set AgentDrift (CC BY 4.0, 12,536 trajectories) was written by Llama-3.3-70B (S54, OWNER, flagged against the "no bigger teacher" rule).
3. **The realistic ALLOW-R5 pool is small, general-safety, and mostly attack-only.** After the refuter pass (18 Oct 2026) it is: HackAPrompt (human prompts, program-checked success label, MIT, 600k-scale; S40), Gandalf-ignore-instructions and Gandalf-summarization (human prompts, MIT; S42, S43), Aya Red-teaming (Apache-2.0, human authorship now confirmed; S15), Civil Comments (CC0), Measuring Hate Speech (CC BY 4.0) and HateXplain (MIT) for human toxicity labels (low relevance; S140-S142). The refuter moved verazuo/jailbreak_llms (S21: tag/keyword labels, third-party platform text) and OASST2 (S153: ChatGPT text admitted inside the human messages) to OWNER. Every one of these needs benign counterparts and a G13 pass against the injection-eval literals (G1) before use; most carry no decision-shaped negatives.
4. **Largest safe-looking levers are all OWNER, not ALLOW:** WildGuardMix train (86,759; 87% synthetic, GPT-4 labels; S01), WildJailbreak (261k; GPT-4/Mixtral/GPT-3.5 made; S20), Aegis 2.0 and 1.0 (human prompt labels, LLM-written responses; S02, S03), Anthropic HH-RLHF harmless-base and red-team-attempts (human crowd data, but the card says not meant for fine-tuning; S13). A "prompt-only, human-prompt-label" slice of Aegis 1.0/2.0 would be ALLOW-R5 if the owner accepts HH-RLHF-derived human prompts (consistent with decision_index.md row N-8).
5. **Traps that look permissive but are not:** GuardReasonerTrain says "mit" but is built from BeaverTails and ToxicChat (both CC BY-NC) plus GPT-4o reasoning (S08); PromptShield says "apache-2.0" but is a composite with Alpaca/UltraChat benign text and an NC upstream (S66); Do-Not-Answer has Apache metadata but its text says CC BY-NC-SA (S16); AgentAlign's benign half includes ToolACE (barred, E16) (S105); AgentHarm is MIT plus an explicit "refrain from using examples ... for training" clause (S110); AgentTrust is AGPL-3.0-or-later since v0.6.0 (E55) (S121); the "malicious skill files" benchmark is built from Atomic Red Team commands (E5) (S75).
6. **Recommended registry additions (G13 targets, names only).** The registry lists only WildGuardTest (B9), NotInject (A4/D9), ATBench (A6), R-Judge (C2), TraceSafe (C3) and the injection-environment targets. Public safety benchmarks that guard-model papers report on, and that any C2.1 safety row could accidentally copy, are NOT yet registered: XSTest, OR-Bench, JailbreakBench JBB-Behaviors, HarmBench, StrongREJECT, SORRY-Bench, AgentHarm, ASB, Agent-SafetyBench, ASSEBench, TS-Bench, OS-Harm, SafeArena, RedCode, SABER, Aegis 1.0/2.0 test, ToxicChat test, BeaverTails test, Lakera b3 and PINT, BrowseSafe-Bench test, PromptShield eval split, promptprotection/agent-security-datasets. Register them even though none is used as data, so the 200-row G13 pilot checks against them.
7. **Open owner questions** are in section 9.
