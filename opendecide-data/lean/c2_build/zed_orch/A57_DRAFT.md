> **Ruling A57 (17 Oct 2026, ~02:00 UTC; orchestrator ruling under the owner's "ok do the best things" (go faster
> without losing accuracy); changes only the order of work; no gate, bar or isolation rule is loosened).**
>
> - **(a) Change.** A42-D 13.1 says the Haiku surface text "is done Mon 19 Oct, before any training-side Haiku text of
>   the six families", and FINAL_PLAN P3 and its Mon 19 row say the same. These are read as follows: no training-side
>   Haiku text of T40-T45 is **admitted, frozen into a pool, rendered into any row, or read by any model** before the
>   final OHS-AC surface-text hash is recorded and exported (step 5 below). C2-side authoring and the
>   sealed-independent admission checks (step 4) may run earlier, in parallel with the surface text.
>   - Reason: the binding protection is G13 and G-TPL against the final sealed text. That comparison is the same
>     whether the pool items are written before or after the surface text, because the items are only compared after
>     the final export in both orders.
>   - The order also guarded one other risk: sealed surface text being shaped by training-side text that already
>     exists. Item (d) blocks that risk directly.
>   - FINAL_PLAN's Mon 19 placement followed the Haiku usage reset. That is a usage estimate, and A47 rules that
>     scope and order follow measured usage.
> - **(b) Scope.** Every C2-side Haiku-authored text of T40-T45: header skins, question wordings, delegation
>   phrasings, attack lines, tool descriptions and any other pool or lexicon item, including the dev-only (spec 7.1)
>   partition and top-ups. It does not cover program text, the sealed side's own text, or the 17 existing families
>   (A1 rules, A54).
> - **(c) Held-out roles are not authored on the C2 side after the freeze.**
>   - Some prepared task files plan items in held-out roles: T40 `HELD_OUT_REGISTERS`, and T41 `N_SKIN_HELD`,
>     `N_WORDING_HELD`, `N_DELEG_HELD` and the held-out attack lines.
>   - A held-out-role item written after the programmatic freeze can enter neither the frozen sealed split nor any
>     training or dev row (13.1). So such items are never authored on the C2 side.
>   - Before dispatch, these task files are regenerated by program with every held-out-role quota at 0. Training and
>     dev-only roles are unchanged. The task files' sha256 values are logged.
>   - Any held-out-role answer that exists anyway is deleted unused and logged by count.
> - **(d) Isolation both ways.**
>   - **C2 side.** Pool authors are fresh Haiku agents, each with one task file. They have no access to `ohs*` paths,
>     `*sealed*` paths or `c2_build/sealed_hashes_ac/`, and they never receive checker results. Every transcript passes
>     `zed_orch/audit_cc_reads.py`, where the only allowed file is the agent's own task file. A failed audit discards
>     that agent's answers unused. Answers are written only to `c2_build/a57_pool_quarantine/<family>/`. No loader,
>     import, row or model read touches that folder before step 6.
>   - **Sealed side.** Every sealed surface-text prompt (paraphrase variants 1 and 4, hijack tiers 2-3) adds these to
>     the AC_SEALED_BRIEF 1 never-open list: `c2_build/a57_pool_quarantine/**`, `c2_build/families/T4*/haiku/**`,
>     `families/T41/haiku/author/**` and any T40-T45 pool or lexicon answer file. A sealed-side access audit reports
>     only the number of such opens. The required number is 0. If it is not 0, the sealed side discards that agent's
>     surface text and redoes it with a fresh agent, and reports only counts.
> - **(e) Binding order of steps.** Each step starts only when the one before it is complete. Steps 3 and 4 run in
>   parallel with the sealed surface text.
>   1. **Freeze in place.** The OHS-AC programmatic freeze is done and its PREREG addendum is written (manifest sha256
>      and the A42-D sha `cbdc29d9…`). The freeze-time hash export in `c2_build/sealed_hashes_ac/` is complete in the
>      FORMAT.md layout: 10- and 12-gram `.npy`, text, structure and keyword hashes, with every held-out
>      registry/tool/carrier name and real-text source identifier, plus `MANIFEST.json` and `FORMAT.md`. The run-only
>      checkers `g13_ohsac_check.py` and `gtpl_check_ac.py` exist and pass `--selftest` (exit 0).
>   2. **Task files.** Apply (c). Then check every task-file text against the freeze-time export on the C2 side
>      (n-gram 10/12, full-text and sentence hashes, keyword hashes as FORMAT.md describes). The result must be 0
>      hits. A hit means a program fix to the task generator, never a manual edit. Then log the task-file sha256
>      manifest.
>   3. **Authoring.** Run under (d). Answers go to the quarantine folder only.
>   4. **Sealed-independent admission.** Drops only, never a rewrite.
>      - The family's own admission checker (`admit_*` / `check_*`), G-SL, G-LEX 2/2 blind Haiku reads, and the
>        family's negation and fact-lock checks.
>      - G13-seed, stage 1, against every G13 registry target that exists now: OHS-4 (its hashes and checkers), the
>        OHS-AC freeze-time export and checkers, public anchors, the G-proxy, the dev targets under A53, and the
>        held-out keyword hashes.
>      - A pass at this step admits nothing.
>   5. **Final sealed text recorded.**
>      - The sealed side finishes the surface text, and the final OHS-AC hash is recorded in a PREREG addendum (13.1).
>      - The sealed side re-exports `c2_build/sealed_hashes_ac/` from the final suite (base items plus all surface
>        variants and rewrites) in the same format. Its `MANIFEST.json` must name the final manifest sha256 that the
>        addendum records.
>      - Both checkers verify that final manifest and pass `--selftest`.
>      - If the re-export does not contain the freeze-time hashes, the union of the two exports binds.
>   6. **Stage-2 admission.** Run on every item that survived step 4, and on any task-file text that a model will see.
>      Each check counts only if its result JSON shows `manifest_verified: true`, the final suite sha and
>      `complete: true`. Exit 3 or an incomplete result admits nothing.
>      - (i) The C2-side hash compare against the final export: `ngram10`, `ngram12`, `full_text_norm`,
>        `full_text_norm_min5words`, `full_text_raw`, `sentence_norm_min5words`, the structure kinds and `keyword`.
>      - (ii) `g13_ohsac_check.py --rows`, with each item as its own unit (its text in `state`, `call_id` = item id),
>        with every method binding: exact_full, exact_seg, exact_short, substring, ngram10/12, tulu8, minhash and
>        structure_g13.
>      - (iii) `gtpl_check_ac.py --templates` on every slotted item and every template, at `--min-shared 1` and
>        `--parts all`.
>      - Any hit by any method drops the item, or the whole unit. A dropped item is never reworded or resubmitted,
>        because that would turn the checker into a probe. The CHECKERS.md "reword and run again" step does not apply
>        to pool items. The checkers are run only, never opened. They run once per authored batch.
>   7. **Pool freeze and the normal chain.** Freeze the admitted pools (sha256 logged), then write the pool loaders.
>      Only then do the rows follow (pilot, G6 QC, probe, own dev, candidates), with every A42-D 3 gate. G13 then
>      runs on candidates and on the final mix, against the final export, with 0 hits after drops (3.5).
> - **(f) Top-ups.**
>   - A pool below its minimum (for example T41's ≥ 6 per form, or T44's ≥ 50 wordings) may be topped up only by
>     fresh Haiku agents. They use the same, unchanged task prompt, are given no hit content, count or id, and their
>     items go through steps 3-6 in full.
>   - A shortfall that remains lowers the family's pool. Nothing is refilled by hand.
> - **(g) Unchanged.**
>   - Deadlines: pilot verdicts by Mon 19 Oct 23:59 UTC (1.6, 3.6). If step 5 has not happened in time, no pool item
